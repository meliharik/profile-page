Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Melih Arik (hereinafter referred to as "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects the personal information you provide when using the Smart CV application (hereinafter referred to as "the Application").

Information We Collect

When using the Application, we may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Full name
  • Address
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Work experience
  • Education history
  • Certifications
  • Projects
  • Native language and foreign languages

Use of Collected Information

The information you provide is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To generate and populate CV and cover letter templates.
  • To facilitate AI-powered interview simulations.
  • To translate CVs and cover letters into 12 languages.

Data Storage and Security

Data Storage

Your personal data is not stored on our servers or in any external cloud databases. All information remains on your device and is utilized solely for the creation of requested documents.

Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal information while it is on your device, ensuring that it remains confidential and secure.

Data Sharing and Retention

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties. No data is transmitted or stored beyond your device, and no information is used for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for the duration necessary to complete the document creation process. Once this process is complete, your data is no longer accessible, as it is not stored externally.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and it is your responsibility to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
