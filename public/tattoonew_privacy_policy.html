Image Processing and Storage

We want to be completely transparent about how we handle your images:

Our Image Handling Process:

  1. Upload: When you select an image, it is securely transmitted to our servers
  2. Processing: The image is processed by our AI models on our secure servers to generate your tattoo design
  3. Delivery: The generated tattoo design is sent back to your device
  4. Deletion: Your original image and all processing data are immediately and automatically deleted from our servers
  5. Storage: The final result is stored only on your device; we retain no copies

We do not:

  • Store your images on our servers after processing
  • Use your images to train our AI models
  • Share your images with any third parties
  • Create backups or archives of uploaded images
  • Use your images for any purpose other than generating your requested tattoo design
