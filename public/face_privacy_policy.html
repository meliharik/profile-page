3. Facial Data Processing

We take the processing of facial data extremely seriously and implement strict measures to protect your privacy:

3.1 Data Processing Principles

  • Local Processing: All facial recognition and face swapping operations are performed locally on your device
  • Temporary Storage: Facial features are only stored temporarily during the face swapping process
  • Data Minimization: We only collect and process the minimum amount of facial data necessary for the face swapping functionality
  • No Biometric Database: We do not maintain a database of facial recognition data or biometric information

3.2 Face Processing Technology

Our face swapping technology:

  • Uses advanced AI algorithms to detect and analyze facial features
  • Creates temporary mathematical representations of facial characteristics
  • Generates face swaps without storing or transmitting raw facial data
  • Automatically deletes all intermediate processing data after swap generation

3.3 Generated Content Storage

For generated face-swapped images:

  • Images are stored securely in your personal account
  • You maintain full control over your generated content
  • You can delete your generated images at any time
  • Deleted images are permanently removed from our systems

Face Data Collection and Processing

What Face Data We Collect

Our app collects and processes the following face-related data:

  • Face images that you explicitly upload for face swapping
  • Generated face-swapped images created through our service
  • Temporary facial feature data used during the face swapping process

How We Use Face Data

Your face data is used exclusively for:

  • Performing the face swap operation you request
  • Generating the face-swapped image result
  • Storing your generated images in your personal gallery

Face Data Storage and Sharing

We handle your face data with utmost care:

  • Source and target face images are stored locally on your device only
  • Face data is temporarily processed on our secure servers solely for face swapping
  • We do not share your face data with any third parties
  • Face data sent to our servers for processing is encrypted in transit

Face Data Retention

Our face data retention policy is designed to protect your privacy:

  • Source and target images remain on your device until you delete them
  • Server-side face data is automatically deleted after processing is complete
  • Generated face-swapped images are stored locally on your device and can be deleted at any time
  • You can clear all face data from the app through the Settings menu

Your Control Over Face Data

You maintain full control over your face data:

  • You can delete any or all face-swapped images at any time
  • You can clear all app data, including face images, through the Settings
  • You can request complete deletion of any server-side data by contacting us
