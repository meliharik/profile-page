Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 5, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Suby ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our subscription management application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Device Information: Device ID, operating system, and version
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our app, features used, and time spent
  • Subscription Data: Information about your subscriptions (names, amounts, billing dates)
  • Payment Information: We do not store your payment details. All payments are processed through RevenueCat
  • Location Data: Country information for currency and pricing purposes

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our subscription management service
  • Send notifications about your upcoming subscription payments
  • Process your premium subscription purchases
  • Improve our app and develop new features
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Firebase servers. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information.

5. Premium Subscription

Our premium subscription service includes:

  • Pricing varies by region ($5-$20)
  • Available in weekly and quarterly plans
  • Automatic renewal unless cancelled
  • Payments processed through App Store/Google Play

6. Data Sharing

We share data with:

  • RevenueCat: For subscription management
  • Firebase: For data storage and analytics
  • Third-party service providers: For app functionality

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Data portability
