Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2025

This privacy policy sets out how Melih Arik uses and protects any information that you give when you use the Nova AI Video Generator application. Your privacy is very important to us, and we are committed to safeguarding it. Nova transforms your images and text prompts into stunning 5-second AI-generated videos, and we want you to understand how we handle your data.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use Nova AI Video Generator:

  • Uploaded Images: The images you upload to the app for video generation. These images are essential for Nova to create your personalized AI-generated videos. Images are processed through our AI engine to transform them into dynamic video content.
  • Text Prompts: The text descriptions and prompts you provide to guide the AI video generation process. These prompts help our AI understand what kind of video you want to create and are crucial for delivering results that match your creative vision.
  • Generated Videos: The videos created by our AI based on your inputs. We temporarily store these videos to allow you to download and share them. Videos are automatically deleted from our servers after 30 days unless you choose to save them to your device.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the app, including the number of videos generated, features accessed, session duration, and other related usage statistics. This data helps us understand user engagement patterns and improve the app's functionality.
  • Device Information: Information about the device you use to access Nova, including device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and IP address. This information helps us optimize the app for different devices and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Subscription Information: Details about your subscription plan, including the type of subscription (weekly, monthly, yearly, or lifetime) and payment information processed through Apple's App Store. This information is necessary to manage your access to premium features.

How We Use Collected Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Video Generation: To process your images and text prompts using our advanced AI technology to create unique 5-second videos. This is the core functionality of Nova AI Video Generator.
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, feedback, and performance metrics to continually enhance our AI models, improve video quality, and add new features. Understanding how users interact with Nova allows us to make data-driven improvements.
  • Quality Assurance: To monitor the quality of generated videos and ensure our AI models are performing optimally. We may review a small sample of generated content to identify and fix issues.
  • Customer Support: To provide technical support, respond to your inquiries, and help resolve any issues you encounter while using Nova. Your information helps us troubleshoot problems and provide personalized assistance.
  • Marketing and Communications: To send you updates about new features, AI model improvements, special offers, and other relevant information about Nova, if you have opted in to receive such communications.
  • Subscription Management: To manage your subscription plan, process payments through the App Store, and ensure you have access to the features included in your subscription tier.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: To protect our service, users, and infrastructure from unauthorized access, abuse, and fraudulent activities.

AI Processing and Data Usage

Nova uses advanced artificial intelligence models to generate videos from your images and text prompts. Here's what you should know about our AI processing:

  • Processing Time: Your images and prompts are processed in real-time or near-real-time by our AI servers. The processing typically takes a few seconds to a few minutes depending on complexity and server load.
  • Model Training: We do not use your personal images or videos to train our AI models without your explicit consent. However, anonymized and aggregated usage data may be used to improve overall service performance.
  • Content Moderation: Our AI includes content moderation systems to prevent the generation of inappropriate, harmful, or illegal content. If your inputs violate our usage policies, the generation process will be blocked.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • AI Service Providers: We work with third-party AI and cloud computing providers to power Nova's video generation capabilities. These providers have access to your images and prompts only for the purpose of processing them through our AI models. They are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Cloud Storage Providers: We use secure cloud storage services to temporarily store your uploaded images and generated videos. These providers are obligated to protect your data and use it only for storage purposes.
  • Payment Processors: Subscription payments are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your full payment details, and all payment processing is handled according to Apple's privacy policies.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or legal process, or if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new owner. The new owner will be required to honor the commitments made in this Privacy Policy.

Data Storage and Retention

We implement the following data retention practices:

  • Uploaded Images: Temporarily stored during the video generation process and automatically deleted within 24 hours after processing is complete, unless you save them to your device.
  • Generated Videos: Stored on our servers for 30 days to allow you to access and download them. After 30 days, videos are permanently deleted unless you have saved them locally.
  • Account Data: Retained for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with services. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently removed within 90 days.
  • Usage Analytics: Aggregated and anonymized usage data may be retained indefinitely for service improvement purposes.

Security Measures

We take the security of your information seriously and implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL protocols. Stored data is encrypted at rest.
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls ensure that only authorized personnel can access user data, and only when necessary for service provision or support.
  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct regular security assessments and updates to protect against vulnerabilities and emerging threats.
  • Secure Infrastructure: Our servers are hosted in secure data centers with physical and digital security measures in place.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use strong passwords and keep your account credentials confidential.
