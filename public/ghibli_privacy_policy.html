Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Animefy! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using Animefy, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Photos and Images

When you use Animefy, we require access to:

  • Photos you specifically choose to transform
  • Camera access (if you choose to take new photos)
  • Photo library access (to save transformed images)

Technical Data

We automatically collect:

  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App usage statistics
  • Error logs
  • IP address and basic location data

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and improving our AI image transformation service
  • Processing your images with our AI models
  • Technical troubleshooting
  • Analyzing app performance
  • Customizing your experience
  • Providing customer support

Data Storage and Security

Your privacy is important to us. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information. Images are processed using secure cloud services and are temporarily stored only for the duration necessary to complete the transformation process.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services for:

  • Firebase (Analytics and Authentication)
  • RevenueCat (Payment Processing)
  • Replicate (AI Image Processing)

Face Data Usage

What face data does the app collect?

Animefy processes facial features within the images you choose to upload, solely for the purpose of generating stylized AI transformations. This includes detection of facial landmarks, but does not involve biometric identification or facial recognition.

Planned uses of the collected face data

The face data is used exclusively to apply AI-powered style transformations to your images. It is not used for identification, advertising, or any profiling purposes.

Will face data be shared with third parties?

No face data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing or profiling. However, image processing (including face data) is performed securely using third-party service Replicate, solely for transformation purposes.

Where is this information stored?

Face data is temporarily stored in secure cloud environments during the image transformation process and automatically deleted after processing is complete. No permanent storage is performed.

How long is face data retained?

Face data is retained only for the duration necessary to complete the transformation and is then promptly deleted. No data is stored after processing is finished.

Where in the privacy policy is this explained?

Face data practices are covered in the following sections:

  • Information We Collect – under "Photos and Images"
  • How We Use Your Information
  • Data Storage and Security
  • Third-Party Services
